The Phoenix Doctrine is the body of doctrine of the house, and it orders how we understand
transformation: not as a technology migration, but as a deliberate rebuilding of the way a company
operates.

It gives their names to the `SLG_Academy` programmes — `Phoenix PEEx`, `Phoenix TEAx` and
`Phoenix RETx` — and it supplies the judgement with which we assess any initiative: first what stops,
then what starts.

The full document is available on request.
