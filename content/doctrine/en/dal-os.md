DAL OS is the capability system that sustains the doctrine, and it rests on **three pillars**.

**Destrucción Creativa.** What must stop weighs more than what must start. An organisation that only
adds ends up sustaining two ways of working and paying for both.

**Antifragility.** Withstanding the blow is not enough: operations have to come out of it better. It
is the opposite of armouring processes, and it is designed on purpose.

**AI Literacy.** Judgement, not tool handling. Whoever decides does not need to program a model; they
need to know what to ask, what to demand and how to verify what they are promised.
