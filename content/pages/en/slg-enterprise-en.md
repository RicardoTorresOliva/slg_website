**Phases 02 and 03 of the method · Diagnosis and building.** The VoltAi by SLG line that answers two questions in this order: where are we, and how do we get there.

> "I do not know where my company stands, and I suspect internal enthusiasm is not the answer."

The diagnosis is an exhaustive readiness analysis across eleven dimensions, with evidence per dimension and a reasoned order of priority. Implementation executes what that diagnosis prioritised, in phases, with an approval gate of yours before each one.

The order matters. Implementing without a diagnosis means solving the most visible symptom instead of the one that blocks.
