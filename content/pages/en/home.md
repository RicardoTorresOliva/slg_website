## Hero

We help you decide, build, and operate in the age of artificial intelligence — no hype.

SLG_AI diagnoses, trains, and builds. SLG_Holdings creates and structures AI-first business at
international scale.

**SLG_AI** — The artificial intelligence branch: diagnosis, implementation, training, and asset
building. → `/ai`

**SLG_Holdings** — The other branch: creating and structuring AI-first companies with international
reach. → `/holdings`

→ `/ai/academy`

→ `/ai/enterprise`

→ `/ai/factory`
