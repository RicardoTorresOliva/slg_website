This questionnaire helps us understand your starting point before a program begins. It takes 15 to 25 minutes.

It is optional and screens no one out: there are no right answers, no cut-off score, and no one is excluded by what they answer. Leave blank anything you do not know. If you would rather write to us directly, email **support@softlandingglobal.com**.
