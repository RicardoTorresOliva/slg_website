`VoltAi by SLG` is the artificial intelligence axis of the agency, and it runs in **three lines** that answer three different moments.

**`VoltAi Academy`** — build judgement in the people who decide. The Phoenix family programmes, bespoke programmes and individual support for directors.

**`VoltAi Enterprise`** — know where you are and get where you decide to go. `SLG_Readiness` diagnoses; `SLG_Implement` executes what the diagnosis prioritised.

**`VoltAi Factory`** — build what is not in the catalogue. Applications, agents, and operations leadership as a service.

There is no mandatory order, but there is a sensible one: judgement first, then diagnosis, and only then building. Starting at the end is the most expensive way to find out it was not needed.

## Why VoltAi

In 1800, Alessandro Volta introduced the first electric battery—an invention that made sustained electric current possible and opened the door to new uses for electricity. His legacy inspires us through his ability to turn knowledge into a force others could put to work.

**VoltAi is named in his honor.** We bring his name together with AI, artificial intelligence, to express our purpose: turning intelligence into action.

We implement AI to connect people, decisions, and processes, transforming each company’s potential into tangible results. This is how we understand progress: ideas becoming tools, capabilities multiplying, and new opportunities for growth taking shape.
