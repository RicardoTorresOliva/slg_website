`SLG_AI` is the artificial intelligence branch of the house, and it runs in **three lines** that answer three different moments.

**`SLG_Academy`** — build judgement in the people who decide. The Phoenix family programmes, bespoke programmes and individual support for directors.

**`SLG_Enterprise`** — know where you are and get where you decide to go. `SLG_Readiness` diagnoses; `SLG_Implement` executes what the diagnosis prioritised.

**`SLG_Factory`** — build what is not in the catalogue. Applications, agents, and operations leadership as a service.

There is no mandatory order, but there is a sensible one: judgement first, then diagnosis, and only then building. Starting at the end is the most expensive way to find out it was not needed.
