Everything we do comes from a written body of doctrine, and it is written before each engagement, not after.

**The Phoenix Doctrine** gives its name to the `SLG_Academy` programmes and orders how we understand transformation: not as a technology migration, but as a deliberate rebuilding of the way a company operates.

**DAL OS** is the capability system that sustains it, and it rests on **three pillars**:

**Destrucción Creativa.** What must stop weighs more than what must start. An organisation that only adds ends up sustaining two ways of working and paying for both.

**Antifragility.** Withstanding the blow is not enough: operations have to come out of it better. It is the opposite of armouring processes, and it is designed on purpose.

**AI Literacy.** Judgement, not tool handling. Whoever decides does not need to program a model; they need to know what to ask, what to demand and how to verify what they are promised.
