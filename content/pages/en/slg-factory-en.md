**Phase 03 of the method · Building.** The VoltAi by SLG line that builds what is not in the catalogue.

> "We know what needs doing; what is missing is building it without getting locked in."

Bespoke applications that reach production, not demo. Agents that carry out work with delegated judgement and explicit limits. And the operations leadership a growing company needs before it can bring that person onto the payroll.

All of it shares the same method: specification before code, approval gates at every phase, and handover to the team that will maintain it as part of the engagement, not as a parting favour.
