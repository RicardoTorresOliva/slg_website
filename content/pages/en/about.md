**SLG Agency Inc.** is a company incorporated in Florida, United States.

We work with boards and executive committees that have to decide on artificial intelligence and do not want to do it on the information supplied by whoever is selling. Our model is informational: we publish the judgement, we deliver the document, and the conversation comes afterwards if you want it.

**How we work, in four rules we do not negotiate.** No lock-in: nothing we build ties you to us. You choose the stack. Every phase passes through an approval gate of yours. And the capability stays inside your company — if you still depend on us when we finish, the work is not done.

**What we do not do.** We do not resell licences. We take no commission from any vendor. And we do not publish a figure, a case or a client name without written authorisation, which is why you will not find one on these pages.
