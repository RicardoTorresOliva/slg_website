**Cliente Demo** is a strategy consultancy for mid-sized companies. We work in small teams, with no intermediate layers: whoever runs the diagnosis presents the plan.

## Why we exist

Many companies grow faster than the way they make decisions. There comes a point where there are more opportunities than time, and a wrong choice costs a year. Our job is to make sure that choice is made with information.

## How we work

**With your data.** We start from what the company already measures and from what its team says.

**In stages.** Each stage ends with a deliverable you can review before moving on.

**So you do not depend on us.** When we finish, the plan and the method stay in your company.

## Where to start

If you want to see our method before talking, download any of the documents. If you prefer a direct conversation, [write to us](/en/contact).
