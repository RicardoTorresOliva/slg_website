`SLG_AI` is SLG Agency's artificial intelligence branch: diagnosis, implementation, training, and
asset building, organized in three lines.

**SLG_Academy** — Training for whoever decides, manages, or researches with artificial intelligence.
→ `/ai/academy`

**SLG_Enterprise** — Readiness diagnosis and implementation of real use cases.
→ `/ai/enterprise`

**SLG_Factory** — Building digital assets, AI agents, and complete companies.
→ `/ai/factory`
