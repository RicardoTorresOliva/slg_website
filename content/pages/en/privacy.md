## What we collect, and only when you give it

This site **collects no identifiable browsing data and uses no tracking cookies**. There are no
third-party scripts on the public pages: no social networks, no advertising, no heatmaps.

We collect data only when you fill in a form:

- **Corporate email address.** Required. We reject free email domains because we write for a
  specific professional reader.
- **Name, company and job title**, when the form asks for them.
- **Request context**: which document you requested, from which page, in which language, and
  campaign parameters (`utm_*`) if you arrived through a link carrying them.
- **The timestamp of your consent** and the version of this policy in force at that moment.

We collect no special category data, build no automated profiles and make no automated decisions
about you.

## What we use it for

- To **deliver the document** you requested.
- To **get in touch with you** about the SLG Agency services related to that request.

We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties for commercial purposes.

## Where it lives and who else sees it

- In **our own database**, on a server managed by SLG Agency.
- In **Softlanding Global's own CRM**, also self-hosted, where your contact record and a note with
  the context of your request are stored.
- **Resend** acts as transactional email provider and processes the delivery of the emails we send
  you. Open and click tracking is **disabled**.
- **Cloudflare R2** holds encrypted backups. Backups are encrypted before leaving our server.

## Download links

The links we deliver documents with **expire within minutes** and are meant for a single recipient.
We publish no listing of our files and there is no public route that lets anyone browse them.

When we change this policy we will update the date above. With every consent we store the version
that was published at that moment, so it is always possible to know exactly what was consented to.
