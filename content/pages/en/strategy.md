The line of `Consultoría` that brings order to growth decisions.

`Diagnóstico` describes the company's situation with data and conversations with its team. `Plan de crecimiento` turns that diagnosis into priorities, owners and dates.

The order matters: planning without a diagnosis means solving the most visible problem instead of the one holding you back.
