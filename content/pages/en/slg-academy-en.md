`SLG_Academy` brings together SLG Agency's training: the Phoenix Programs, by who takes them, and two
additional formats for needs that don't fit a fixed program.

**Phoenix PEEx** — For senior leadership. → `/en/ai/academy/phoenix-peex`

**Phoenix TEAx** — For senior management. → `/en/ai/academy/phoenix-teax`

**Phoenix RETx** — For researchers and undergraduate/graduate students. → `/en/ai/academy/phoenix-retx`

**Customize Programs** — Training designed to measure. → `/en/ai/academy/customize-programs`

**AI Coaching for Directors** — One-on-one coaching for directors. → `/en/ai/academy/ai-coaching`
