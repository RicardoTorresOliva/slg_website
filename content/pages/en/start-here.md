## Hero

We are an AI-first business transformation agency.

We design, build and deploy businesses for the new business landscape built on artificial intelligence.

## AI changes how reality is seen

AI is not just a tool. It forces a rethink of how we work, of how business models are architected, of digital geography as a market premise, and of how we learn and come to know the world. It does not only change how things are done: it changes how reality is seen.

## The problem we solve

Some companies decide about AI on the information of whoever is selling it, and install it on ways of working that no longer fit the landscape. We redesign the business, not just the tools, and the capability stays inside your company.

## A doctrine and an operating system

We do not improvise: we have a [doctrine](/en/doctrine) that explains why and an operating system, DAL OS, that says how it is carried out. Judgement first, then building.

## AI-first and Human-first

We say AI-first to describe how we build digital assets. Our DNA is Human-first, always: AI amplifies human judgement, it does not replace it, and the decision is yours.
