## Who it is for and what problem

For the director who has to decide on artificial intelligence without having had time to learn it, and who finds that the two available sources — the vendor who sells and the enthusiast who has implemented nothing — are no help in deciding.
The problem is not a lack of information: it is that neither is written from the chair you sit in.

## What it is

An `SLG_Academy` programme of the **Phoenix** family, built on The Phoenix Doctrine.
It is designed for the executive level: it does not teach you to use tools, it teaches you to decide about them — what to ask, what to demand, what not to buy, and how to tell whether what was implemented works.

## What it includes

Working sessions with content prepared around your context, not a generic syllabus.
The three pillars of DAL OS — Destrucción Creativa, Antifragility and AI Literacy — applied to real decisions in your company.
Reference material that stays with you afterwards.
A judgement of your own for evaluating third-party proposals, which is what an executive actually takes away from this programme.

## How we work

No lock-in: nothing we build ties you to us or to a vendor.
You choose the stack, and if you already have one, we work with the one you have.
Every phase passes through an approval gate of yours before the next one starts.
And the capability stays inside your company: if you still depend on us when we finish, the work is not done.
