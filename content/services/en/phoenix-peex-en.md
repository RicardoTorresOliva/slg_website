## Who it is for and what problem

For board members and executive committees who must approve — or stop — artificial intelligence
initiatives without technical training of their own. The problem isn't the technology: it's
deciding with judgment when the team presenting the project knows more than the person approving
it.

## What it is

Phoenix PEEx is the `SLG_Academy` Phoenix Program built for senior leadership: the Phoenix line
meant for those who decide, not those who execute.

## What it includes

- What to ask before approving an AI initiative
- How to tell a serious implementation from a decorative pilot
- What to require in governance, data and capability transfer

## How we work

No lock-in, the client chooses the stack, approval gates, transferable capability.
