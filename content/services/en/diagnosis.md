## Who it is for and what problem

For management teams who feel the company could grow more, but are not sure what is holding it back.
Without a diagnosis, each area defends its own explanation and decisions are made by insistence, not evidence.

## What it is

An orderly review of the company: its numbers, its processes and what its team thinks.
The result is a shared description of the situation, with the three or four brakes that weigh the most.

## What it includes

Interviews with management and with the people who keep operations running.
A review of the indicators the company already tracks, without asking for new systems.
A short report with the prioritised brakes and the evidence for each one.
A presentation session with management to discuss the report.

## How we work

We start from what already exists: your data, your reports and your people.
Every finding comes with its evidence, so it can be discussed and not just accepted.
You review each deliverable before we move on to the next.
