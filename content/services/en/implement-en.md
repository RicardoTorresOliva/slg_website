## Who it is for and what problem

For the company that already knows what it wants to implement and needs it to happen, with operations running and without the project depending on nobody going on holiday.
The usual problem is not starting: it is month four, when the pilot worked and the rollout is not moving.

## What it is

The execution of what the diagnosis prioritised, with approval gates at every phase.
We work on your stack and with your people inside the team, because the goal is not to hand over a system but to leave a capability installed.

## What it includes

A phased plan with explicit exit criteria: each phase closes when it does what it said it would do.
Implementation on the infrastructure you already have, without migrations nobody asked for.
Documented capability transfer: who knows how to operate what, and since when.
An approval gate of yours before each following phase — including the one where you stop.

## How we work

No lock-in: nothing we build ties you to us or to a vendor.
You choose the stack, and if you already have one, we work with the one you have.
Every phase passes through an approval gate of yours before the next one starts.
And the capability stays inside your company: if you still depend on us when we finish, the work is not done.
