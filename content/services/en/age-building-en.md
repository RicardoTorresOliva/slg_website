## Who it is for and what problem

For the organisation that has already automated the obvious and runs into the work that demands judgement and not just rules: what a person does today by reading, deciding and writing.
The problem with automating that with rules is that the first exception breaks the flow and hands the work back to the person, with the irritation added.

## What it is

The **artificial intelligence agent factory** of `VoltAi Factory`: systems that carry out work with delegated judgement, inside limits you define.
They are built like an application — with specification, gates and tests — and with something an application does not need: explicit limits on what the agent may **not** do on its own.

## What it includes

Definition of the agent's scope and, above all, of its limits: what it decides, what it proposes and what it never does without a person.
Guardrails and an operating budget, fixed before it is put to work.
Traceability: every action leaves a record of why it was taken.
Tests on real cases, including those that must end in "this one is not mine to do".
Deployment, supervision and handover to the team that will live with it.

## How we work

No lock-in: nothing we build ties you to us or to a vendor.
You choose the stack, and if you already have one, we work with the one you have.
Every phase passes through an approval gate of yours before the next one starts.
And the capability stays inside your company: if you still depend on us when we finish, the work is not done.
