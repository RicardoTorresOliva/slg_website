## Who it is for and what problem

For companies that already know what holds them back and need to decide what to do first, who does it and with what resources.
A diagnosis that never becomes a plan stays a good document that nobody opens again.

## What it is

A work plan for the next twelve months, built with management from the diagnosis.
It ranks initiatives by impact and effort, and gives each one an owner and a date.

## What it includes

Workshops with management to choose and order the initiatives.
A quarterly roadmap with owners, dates and progress indicators.
A simple dashboard to follow the plan with the tools the company already uses.
Two follow-up reviews during the first months of execution.

## How we work

Your managers decide the plan; we structure the conversation and bring the method.
We prefer a few initiatives done well to a long list nobody can sustain.
Each follow-up review ends with concrete adjustments, not with a report.
