## Who it is for and what problem

For the growing company that needs operations leadership and cannot — or should not — yet bring that person onto the payroll.
The problem with postponing it is familiar: the chief executive ends up running operations, and while running them nobody is steering the company.

## What it is

Operations leadership as a service, from `SLG_Factory`.
It is not consulting that recommends and leaves: it is a figure who operates, accountable for agreed results and present over time.

## What it includes

The service rests on **three promises**, and all three are written into the agreement before starting.
Leadership of the agreed operations, accountable for the result and not only for the advice.
Installation of the way of working: what is left in place has to keep working once the service ends.
A planned exit from the outset, because the goal of this service is to stop being necessary.

## How we work

No lock-in: nothing we build ties you to us or to a vendor.
You choose the stack, and if you already have one, we work with the one you have.
Every phase passes through an approval gate of yours before the next one starts.
And the capability stays inside your company: if you still depend on us when we finish, the work is not done.
