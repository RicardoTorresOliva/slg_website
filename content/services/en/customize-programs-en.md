## Who it is for and what problem

For the company whose starting point fits no catalogue programme, because its sector, its regulation or its maturity place it outside the general case.
The problem with a standard programme here is not that it is bad: it is that half of it is surplus and the other half is missing.

## What it is

An `SLG_Academy` programme **designed around your context**, not adapted from a template.
It is built from the same doctrine and the same pillars as the rest of the line, but scope, audience and depth are decided with you before starting.

## What it includes

A scoping conversation beforehand, at no cost, to find out whether a bespoke programme is needed or one of the existing ones is enough — and to say so even when the answer is the latter.
Design of the programme: audience, duration, cases and depth.
Delivery to the same standard as the Phoenix family programmes.
Reference material that stays inside your company.

## How we work

No lock-in: nothing we build ties you to us or to a vendor.
You choose the stack, and if you already have one, we work with the one you have.
Every phase passes through an approval gate of yours before the next one starts.
And the capability stays inside your company: if you still depend on us when we finish, the work is not done.
