## Who it is for and what problem

For the organisation whose starting point fits no catalogue programme, because its sector, its regulation or its maturity puts it outside the general case.
The problem with a standard programme here is not that it is bad: it is that half of it is surplus and the other half is missing.

## What it is

An `VoltAi Academy` programme **designed on your context**, not adapted from a template.
It starts from the same doctrine and the same pillars as the rest of the line; scope, audience and depth are decided with you before it begins.

## What it includes

A scoping conversation beforehand to establish whether a bespoke programme is needed or whether Phoenix PEEx, TEAx or RETx is enough — and to say so even when the answer is the second.
Programme design: audience, duration, working cases and depth.
Delivery to the same standard as the Phoenix family programmes.
Reference material that stays inside your organisation.

## How we work

No lock-in: nothing we build ties you to us or to a vendor.
You choose the stack, and if you already have one, we work with the one you have.
Every phase passes through an approval gate of yours before the next one starts.
And the capability stays inside your company: if you still depend on us when we finish, the work is not done.
