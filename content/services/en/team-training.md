## Who it is for and what problem

For companies that have a plan and need their teams to put it into practice every week.
When only management understands the method, the plan depends on a few people and stops as soon as they are away.

## What it is

Hands-on workshops for middle managers and operations teams, held at the company itself.
We work with the team's real data and cases, not textbook examples.

## What it includes

A preliminary conversation with management to fit the workshop to its plan.
Half-day practical sessions, in small groups.
Reference material the team keeps afterwards.
A review session one month later, to clear up the questions that come up in practice.

## How we work

Each session ends with an exercise applied to a real case from the team.
The material stays in your company and can be reused with new people.
If the team still needs us for the basics when we finish, the workshop did not do its job.
