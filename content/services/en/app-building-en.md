## Who it is for and what problem

For the company that needs a specific application and has found that the software catalogue does not have it, or has it in exchange for rebuilding its operation around the product.
The problem with building it in-house is usually the same: you can start, and you can finish; sustaining it for two years is another matter.

## What it is

Bespoke application building in `SLG_Factory`.
What is delivered is not a demo but a system in production, with its deployment, its tests and its documentation — and with the code in a repository that is yours from day one.

## What it includes

Specification before code: what it has to do and how you will know it does it, written down.
Building with approval gates, not with surprise deliveries at the end.
Deployment on the infrastructure you choose, including your own.
Automated tests and operating documentation, which is what separates a system from a prototype.
Handover to the team that will maintain it, with as much accompaniment as it takes.

## How we work

No lock-in: nothing we build ties you to us or to a vendor.
You choose the stack, and if you already have one, we work with the one you have.
Every phase passes through an approval gate of yours before the next one starts.
And the capability stays inside your company: if you still depend on us when we finish, the work is not done.
