## Who it is for and what problem

For the leadership that needs to know, before investing, where its company actually stands in the age of artificial intelligence — and suspects the answer is not the one internal enthusiasm gives.
The problem with starting without a diagnosis is that you end up solving the most visible symptom instead of the one that blocks.

## What it is

An **exhaustive analysis of readiness for the age of artificial intelligence**.
It does not measure technology: it measures the organisation's capacity to adopt it — data, processes, governance, talent and decision-making — and delivers a starting point you can defend in front of a committee.

## What it includes

The diagnosis covers **eleven dimensions**, and all eleven are delivered: there is no summary that hides the one that came out badly.
Evidence per dimension: what each assessment rests on, so it can be argued with.
A reasoned order of priority, with what has to be resolved **before** implementing anything.
A document presentable to a board, free of vendor language.

## How we work

No lock-in: nothing we build ties you to us or to a vendor.
You choose the stack, and if you already have one, we work with the one you have.
Every phase passes through an approval gate of yours before the next one starts.
And the capability stays inside your company: if you still depend on us when we finish, the work is not done.
