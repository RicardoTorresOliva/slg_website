## Who it is for and what problem

For companies of any size who need to know how ready they are for the AI era before investing in
it. The problem isn't a lack of AI initiatives: it's not knowing, with data, where the company
actually stands today.

## What it is

`SLG_Readiness` is a complete, exhaustive analysis that determines how ready a company of any size
is for the artificial intelligence era.

## What it includes

- Organizational architecture
- Communication cohesion
- Process maturity
- Open AI knowledge
- Closed AI knowledge (Shadow AI)
- Risk exposure level
- Key profiles (which ones, whether the company has them or not)
- Market comparison
- Business opportunities
- What-If scenarios
- Interactive web report

No lock-in, the client chooses the stack, approval gates, transferable capability.
